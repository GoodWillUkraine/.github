# GoodWill Tech

**TOP RPA Automation Consultant** (recognized by Clutch) — delegating repetitive work to robots since day one.

## What We Do

GoodWill Tech is a Kyiv-based RPA (Robotic Process Automation) company. We design, build, and maintain automation robots — mostly on UiPath — that take over repetitive back-office work so people don't have to do it manually:

- **Invoice & Finance Automation** — invoice processing, bank statement reconciliation, tax document workflows
- **Logistics & Document Automation** — customs and shipping paperwork, document classification and parsing
- **Multi-System Reporting** — syncing data and status updates across internal and external systems
- **Process Consulting** — in-depth analysis of manual workflows to find what's worth automating
- **RPA Support & Maintenance** — keeping deployed robots running, updated, and adapted as processes change

## Impact

One automation we built cut invoice-processing time by **70%** for a client — a typical result when repetitive manual work moves to a robot.
